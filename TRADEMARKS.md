## Allowed without asking

1. **Unmodified redistribution** of an official release or of the unmodified source, under the
   name and logo, as long as you do not suggest that you are the project or that your
   distribution is endorsed by it.
2. **Nominative / truthful references**: "based on HS Studio", "a fork of HS Studio",
   "compatible with HS Studio", "plugin for HS Studio", reviews, articles, tutorials, talks and
   package-manager entries describing the official software.
3. **Building and running modified versions privately** or within your organization.
4. Linking to the project with its name, and showing the logo next to such a link in its
   original form and colors, only to refer to the official project.

## Not allowed without written permission

1. **Distributing a modified version** (a fork, a patched build, a build with other default
   settings, models or components) under the name "HS Studio", "VHO", or a confusingly similar
   name — including names starting with "HS" for a media/photo/video application — or with the
   logo or a confusingly similar one.
2. Using the marks in the name of your own product, company, domain, app-store listing or social
   media account in a way that suggests it is the official project.
3. Using the marks for unrelated or misleading products, or in a way that suggests endorsement.

## If you fork

Rename the application and replace the icons before you distribute it. In practice:

- change the product name, window title, executable name, bundle identifier and installer
  name, and the "About" text;
- replace the files in `src-tauri/icons/` and `src/assets/` that carry the logo;
- keep the copyright notices, LICENSE and NOTICE (Apache-2.0 section 4 requires them in your
  distribution), and you may say
  "based on HS Studio" with a link to the project.
